TOPIC: 5 AI tools for answering customer emails faster
PILLAR: Shortlist

SLIDE 1 (hook):
You're losing hours a week to customer emails you could answer in one click.

SLIDE 2:
Front + AI drafts
Turns your inbox into a shared queue and auto-drafts replies from your past
answers. Best for teams of 2+ sharing support email.

SLIDE 3:
Gmail's "Help me write"
Built into Gmail, free with Workspace. Drafts a reply from a one-line
prompt. Caveat: generic tone unless you edit it.

SLIDE 4:
Claude or ChatGPT + saved prompt
Paste the customer's email, get a drafted reply in your brand voice using
a saved prompt template. Free to start, fastest to set up.

SLIDE 5:
Intercom Fin
AI resolves common questions automatically before they hit your inbox.
Best if you get repetitive questions (pricing, hours, returns).

SLIDE 6:
Missive AI
Shared inbox + AI drafting for small teams, cheaper than Front. Good
middle ground if Front feels like overkill.

SLIDE 7 (CTA):
Save this for the next time your inbox gets away from you.

CAPTION:
Customer emails eat more time than almost anything else in a small
business — and most of them are variations on the same five questions.
These five tools all do the same core job (draft a reply so you're editing,
not writing from scratch) but fit different setups: solo founders, small
teams, and high-volume support inboxes. Start with whichever one matches
your current email volume, not the fanciest option. Which one are you
already using?
