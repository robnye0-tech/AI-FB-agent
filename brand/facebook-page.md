# Facebook Page Setup

## Page name
`The AI Shortlist`

## Page category
Choose **"Digital creator"** (or **"Business service"** if that option fits
better in the category picker) — matches the Instagram account category so
the two stay consistent once they're linked.

## Short bio / description (255 character limit)

This is the text that shows in Facebook search results and in the Page's
Intro box.

> The AI Shortlist helps small business owners and solo entrepreneurs cut
> through the noise on AI tools. Every post is one clear pick: what it
> does, who it's for, and what it costs — no hype, just what's worth your
> time.

(218 characters — fits with room to spare.)

## About / Page story (longer section, no strict limit)

This goes in the Page's "About" or "Story" section, where Facebook allows
more space than the short bio.

> **What we do**
> The AI Shortlist is a straightforward, no-hype guide to AI tools for
> people running a small business or working solo. New tools launch every
> week, most of them aren't worth your time, and almost none of them come
> with an honest explanation of who they're actually for. We fix that.
>
> Every post covers one tool, one comparison, or one mistake to avoid —
> written in plain language, with real pricing, so you can decide in under
> a minute whether it's worth trying. No jargon, no affiliate hype
> dressed up as advice, no "game-changing" headlines.
>
> If you run a business and don't have time to test every AI tool that
> comes out, that's exactly who this page is for.

## Mission (if the Page setup asks for one separately)

> Help small business owners make confident, fast decisions about AI
> tools — without wasting time or money finding out the hard way.
