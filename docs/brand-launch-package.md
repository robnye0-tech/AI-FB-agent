## 2. V1.0 Core Scope Definition

**Core Offer** — Done-for-you AI setup that solves the #1 pain point for local
service businesses: missed leads and slow follow-up. An AI phone/text
answering + booking assistant that captures every inquiry, answers common
questions, and books the appointment automatically — even after hours.

**Primary Audience** — Owner-operator local service businesses with 1–10
employees: contractors (plumbers, electricians, HVAC), salons/spas, and
gyms/studios. They get steady inbound calls/texts, lose revenue on every
missed one, have no time to build automation themselves, and can immediately
see the ROI.

**Clear Promise** — "Never miss another lead — we set up AI to answer,
respond, and book for you, so you can stay focused on the work."

## 3. Brand Voice

**Fixed — Core Tone Attributes**
- Direct, no-fluff — busy owners, skeptical of tech speak
- Blue-collar respect — sound like someone who's been in a shop, not a boardroom
- ROI-first — every claim ties back to leads saved, jobs booked, hours freed
- Calm confidence — no hype, no jargon, no "AI revolution" talk

**Flexible — How Voice Can Extend**
- Vertical-specific examples can swap in (missed plumbing call vs. missed
  class sign-up) while tone stays the same
- Outreach can be punchier; the website can be slightly warmer to build trust
- Stays casual-professional across every channel — never corporate-stiff,
  never meme-y

## 4. Visual System

**Core Colors**

| Deep Navy | Signal Orange | Warm White | Charcoal |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#1B2A41` | `#FF6B35` | `#F7F5F2` | `#2E2E2E` |

**Typography**
- Headlines: Inter Bold or Archivo — confident, easy to skim on mobile
- Body: Inter Regular / system sans-serif — clean and fast-loading

**Extension Rules**
- New verticals get an accent-color swap only (e.g. teal `#2A9D8F` for gyms) —
  navy, charcoal, and white never change
- One accent color per vertical page, maximum
- Use real photos of trucks, tools, salons, gyms — never stock "AI robot" imagery

## 6. V1.0 Website Copy

**Headline** — Never Miss Another Job Because You Couldn't Get to the Phone

**Subheadline** — We set up AI to answer every call and text, book the
appointment, and follow up — automatically, even after hours. You focus on
the work. It handles the rest.

**Value Props**
- Every Call Answered — no voicemail, no "we'll call you back," instant
  response day or night
- Booked Automatically — appointments land straight on your calendar, no
  back-and-forth
- Set Up in Days, Not Months — we handle setup, no tech skills needed on
  your end

**Primary CTA** — Book a Free Setup Call → ("See exactly how it'd work for
your business — no commitment.")

**Trust Line** — Built for contractors, salons, and gyms who are too busy
doing the work to chase down every missed call.

## 7. 30-Day Content System

- **Week 1 — Cost of a Missed Call.** Simple ROI math content: missed calls
  per week × average job value = money walking out the door. Works as a
  post, carousel, or cold-email opener.
- **Week 2 — Behind-the-Scenes Setup.** Short videos/posts showing the AI
  assistant answering and booking a sample call — demystifies the tech and
  builds trust.
- **Week 3 — Local Business Call-Outs.** Direct, personalized outreach and
  content aimed at specific local contractors, salons, and gyms.
- **Week 4 — Early Social Proof.** Once the first 1–2 clients are live,
  capture a simple before/after result — seeds the future `/results` page.
